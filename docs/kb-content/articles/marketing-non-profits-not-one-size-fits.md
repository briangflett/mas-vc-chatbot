## Marketing for non-profits: not a “one size fits all”

- October 24, 2015
- [BY\\
Leila MacDonald](https://www.masadvise.org/author/leila-macdonald/)

![](https://www.masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Medium-organizations.jpg)

[![](https://masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Marketing-your-non-profit-300x101.jpg)](https://masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Marketing-your-non-profit.jpg)

You may believe that the same marketing approach will fit all non-profits regardless of size.

While there are similarities when developing marketing strategies, effective and efficient marketing depends on your marketing & fundraising spend as indicated in the table below.

| Annual spend on marketing & fundraising (including staff time) | Category |
| --- | --- |
| <$50,000 | Small non-profit with fairly predictable marketing needs |
| $50,000 - $499,000 | Mid-size non-profit that hires or would consider hiring part-time fundraising staff |
| $500,000+ | Large non-profit with a marketing team whose roles align with board members and volunteers |

## Marketing best practices for small non-profits

### Common marketing challenge: “Help us, we have nothing!” [![](https://masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Small-biz-marketing-image-300x181.jpg)](https://masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Small-biz-marketing-image.jpg)

- **Communication touchpoints:** Determine your communication touchpoints based on your target audience.
- **Website:** Ensure your website is aligned with your brand, mission, values and strategic objectives.


It should have plain language, images that align with your brand and simple navigation.
- **Consistent messaging:** Align the content and design from your website with your communication materials.  This helps ensure your messages and look & feel are consistent across all your communications.
- **Annual audit:** Audit your communication pieces each year and if required, update and clarify the content.

## Marketing best practices for medium non-profits

### _Common marketing challenge: “Help us work smarter” ![](https://masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Medium-organizations.jpg)_

- **Communication touchpoints:** Determine your communication touchpoints based on your target audience.
- **Website:** Ensure your website is aligned with your brand, mission, values and strategic objectives. It should have plain language, images that align with your brand and simple navigation.
- **Consistent messaging:** Align the content and design from your website with your communication materials.  This helps ensure your messages and look & feel are consistent across all your communications.
- **Revenue Efficiency Report:** Customize your [Revenue Efficiency Report](http://marketingfornonprofits.ca/marketing-best-practices/annual-reports/revenue-efficiency/) template for your board to highlight the cost effectiveness of your revenue streams and methods for improving your [fundraising ratio](http://www.cra-arc.gc.ca/chrts-gvng/chrts/plcy/cgd/fndrsng-eng.html)


for the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).
- **Donor Churn Report:** Customize your [Donor Churn Report](http://marketingfornonprofits.ca/marketing-best-practices/annual-reports/donor-churn-one2one/) template for your board to identify your effectiveness at acquiring new donors and retaining current donors.

## Marketing best practices for large non-profits

### _Common marketing challenge: “Help us work better” [![](https://masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Large-organizations-300x147.jpg)](https://masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Large-organizations.jpg)_

- **Clarify and protect your brand** The art of marketing is the art of branding. Board members, executive directors and other management all have a role in championing your brand.

- **Calculate your financial metrics**

Prepare the following reports each year. These reports highlight your operating efficiency, the effectiveness of your marketing strategies and year-to-year changes in the efficiency of your marketing efforts.

[Revenue Efficiency Report](http://marketingfornonprofits.ca/marketing-best-practices/annual-reports/revenue-efficiency/) indicates the cost effectiveness of each revenue stream and methods for improving your [fundraising ratio](http://www.cra-arc.gc.ca/chrts-gvng/chrts/plcy/cgd/fndrsng-eng.html) for the CRA.

[Donor Churn Report](http://marketingfornonprofits.ca/marketing-best-practices/annual-reports/donor-churn-one2one/) measures your annual donor portfolio turnover and illustrates your effectiveness

at acquiring new donors and retaining current donors.

[Marketing Effectiveness Report](http://marketingfornonprofits.ca/marketing-best-practices/annual-reports/donor-churn-mass/) identifies marketing activities that are working well and those which

are not as effective.

- **Review your team**

Make sure you have people with the skills and fit required for your team. Ensure you have the proper combination

of marketing generalists and specialists required for your marketing campaigns.
