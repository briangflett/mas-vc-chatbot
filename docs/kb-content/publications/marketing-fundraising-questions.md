Marketing questions 
☐   Can your website be understood in <30 seconds? 
•  Does your navigation bar self-explain your programs? 
•  Is every webpage accessible from your navigation bar?  
•  Does every page have enough subheads/bullets that readers can scan in 8 seconds? 
•  Is the readability of your text at a grade 9 level? 

☐ Does your website clearly explain what you do? 
•  Does your main webpage answer who/what/where/when/why/how?  
•  Do you have pages that answer the 3 main donor questions? (Why should I donate to 
your charity?  What is your track record?  What impact will my donation make?) 

☐Are all marketing tools consistent with the website? 
•  Text - are your messages consistent?  
•  Visuals - do all marketing tools look like a family? 
•  Do you spend enough time on your website vs your annual report and newsletters? 

Fundraising questions 
☐  Do you know which are your cost-efficient donors? 
•  Do you have a diversity of donors (foundations, corporations, major and regular donors)? 

☐  Does your fundraising committee have a variety of skills? 
•  Do you have volunteers who can research, write letters, update files or meet donors? 
•  Do you have a Fundraising Plan that outlines each volunteer’s tasks?  
•  Do you meet regularly to track progress and learn from each other? 

☐  Do you have the tools? 
•  Do you have the 4 main marketing tools (brochure, donor presentation, donor letter 

template, elevator speeches)? 

•  Do you have a prospecting database separate from your donor database? 
•  Do you have an inventory of ideas to present to potential donors?
