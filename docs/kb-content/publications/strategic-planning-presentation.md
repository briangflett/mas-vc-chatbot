# 20 of 40

HOW TO WRITE YOUR VISION AND MISSION

​

- There is no magic formula
- Get input from a large group
- Sit with your board or a sub-committee to craft the statements
- Write, edit, and edit some more
- Circulate it. Sleep on it.
- Revise once again.

Lessons from strategic planning

- Do upfront work
- Ensure a clear mission, vision, and values
- The Board needs to own it
- Address critical strategic issues
- Be strategic – not operational
- Include an operations / action plan
- Get a facilitator
