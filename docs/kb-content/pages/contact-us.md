# Pro Bono Consulting for Not-For-Profit Organizations Across Canada

We have been helping not-for-profit groups like yours for more than 25 years. We would love to help you too. Not sure where to get started? Please read the options below carefully and choose the option best for you.

## Not Sure Where to Start?

MAS offers three main kinds of help:

**Quick Help** when you have a question, need a resource or a brief presentation for Board or staff.  Contact [info@masadvise.org](mailto:info@masadvise.org) or call 416-963-5792.

**Consulting Services** provided by highly skilled and  experienced volunteers who will work with you to define and then undertake a longer term project addressing issues like strategic planning, review and improvement of governance, HR, finance management, or adopting more relevant and successful communication strategies, grappling with technology issues etc.  (Fill in Request Form below).

**Workshops and Facilitation**. Check out our Wednesday Workshop series (click **[here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1U5vTxJUM-zg34sRhcfnn9ExiLMppKyJQ/edit?usp=drive_link&ouid=116072410516957456616&rtpof=true&sd=true)**) or request delivery of a workshop on Board Governance, Advocacy, Marketing/Communications, Organizational Sustainability, Volunteer Engagement etc.

If you need help but don’t know where to begin, please [request a call](https://www.masadvise.org/please-call-me/) with our Client Services Manager. You can also give us a call or send us an email.
