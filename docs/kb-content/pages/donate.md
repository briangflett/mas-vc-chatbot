## Donate today and help us to do more

MAS has been working with not-for-profit organizations for more than 20 years, and is proud to have helped over 1,500 different groups do more for the people they serve.

We wouldn’t be able to do the important work that we do without our team of dedicated and passionate volunteers. But we also wouldn’t be able to help not-for-profit organizations without the support and funding of donors, like you.

![](https://www.masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/management-advisory-service_donate.jpg)

Your gifts help us to fund our office staff and other overhead expenses that are integral to supporting our Volunteer Consultants who donate their time and their expertise to serve our clients.

Your donation can go a long way to helping us provide much needed services, while allowing you to play an active role in supporting not-for-profit organizations in our community.

MAS is registered with CanadaHelps to safely and securely process donations online.
