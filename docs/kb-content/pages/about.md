**MAS and CMC Canada Partner to Expand Free Consulting for Nonprofits**

**Toronto, ON – May 27, 2025** – MAS of Toronto and CMC Canada’s Pro Bono Program have formed a strategic partnership to broaden access to free consulting for nonprofits across Canada. This collaboration brings together MAS’s experienced Volunteer Consultants and Certified Management Consultants (CMCs) to help organizations improve strategy, operations, and impact.

The partnership strengthens both groups’ ability to address challenges in the nonprofit sector and deliver high-impact, pro bono support. Services include strategic advice, workshops, and mentorship, all aimed at helping nonprofits grow sustainably.

To learn more, visit the [CMC Canada Pro Bono Program page](https://www.cmc-canada.ca/Pro-Bono-Consulting-Program).

## Our Mission, Vision & Values

MAS provides volunteer consulting to help sustain and grow Canada’s not-for-profit sector, especially organizations supporting marginalized, underserved and systemically excluded communities.

Our vision is to create a more inclusive, equitable and just Canadian society supported by a vibrant not-for-profit sector.

### Commitment to Our Clients

MAS is committed to excellence in consulting services to our not-for-profit clients and leveraging our knowledge and expertise to address systemic issues.

### Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

MAS will reflect our communities. MAS actively supports diversity, equity and inclusion for our volunteers and clients and is committed to eliminating discrimination.

### Volunteerism

MAS is driven by high quality volunteers. MAS is committed to sustaining an engaged volunteer community by providing challenging and rewarding opportunities to contribute to society and develop MAS’ collective expertise.

### Continuous Learning

MAS is committed to leveraging and growing the wealth of knowledge amongst our volunteers, clients and partners in order to continuously upgrade our offerings and share what we learn with the not-for-profit sector.
