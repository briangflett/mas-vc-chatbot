## Governance & Board Development

Effective governance is crucial to the success of your organization. And over time, as your organization changes, your governance structures and processes may need to change too.

[Get Pro Bono Help](https://www.masadvise.org/contact-us)

- See how MAS can help »

How do you know if you need to make changes to your governance procedures? Ask yourself these questions:

- Does your Board have a clear understanding of its core governance functions?
- Does it have sufficient focus on governance issues compared to operational issues?
- Does it have a plan for improving the governance of your organization?
- Is it focused on the key issues and operating effectively?

If you answered NO to any of these questions, it might be time to take a look at your Board’s operation and consider areas for improvement.

### Your Dedicated Volunteer Consultant

A MAS Volunteer Consultant, who has an expertise in this area, will work together with your Board to:

- Establish a common framework for governance
- Examine various governance models and options
- Help a Board of Directors understand what governance practices it should be engaged in
- Identify related by-laws issues for Board and legal consideration
- Clarify roles and responsibilities
- Improve Board recruitment, orientation, training and retention
- Increase the effectiveness of Board operations
- Maximize the value of Board committees and other supporting structures
- Evaluate Board dynamics

Contact MAS today and let our experienced team work with you. We’ll support you through a governance review process designed to strengthen your Board’s ability to take on whatever challenges you are currently facing, as well as those that may arise in the future.
