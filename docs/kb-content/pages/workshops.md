## Come together to share, network and learn

### Workshops

Many organizations can learn a great deal from others in similar roles, even if they are from different areas of expertise. MAS believes in bringing people together to share information, network, and learn from its own and other experts.

### MAS can help

Periodically, MAS hosts breakfast, half-day or day-long workshops in downtown Toronto that focus on best practices and pressing issues for nonprofits.

Typically, after a presentation that lays the groundwork for the main topic, experts will present on their topic of expertise. There is always time for networking, brainstorming and Q & A.

Registration is limited so that those who attend have an opportunity to mix and mingle as well as listen and learn. Refreshments are included in the cost.

We price our workshops on a break-even basis.
