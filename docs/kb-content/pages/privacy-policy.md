## What Information We Collect

When you use our AI assistant, we collect:

- Your messages and questions
- Our AI responses
- Basic usage information (when you use the service)
- Technical data needed to operate the service

We do NOT collect personal information unless you choose to share it in your messages.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide AI-powered responses and assistance
- Improve the quality of our service
- Maintain technical functionality
- Ensure user safety

## Data Sharing

We use third-party AI services (like OpenAI) to power our assistant. These services have their own privacy policies:

- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy/)

We do NOT:

- Sell your data
- Share your conversations with other users
- Use your data for advertising

## Data Retention

- Conversations may be temporarily stored to maintain context during your session
- Data may be retained for up to 30 days for service improvement
- You can request deletion of your data at any time

## Your Rights

You can:

- Request access to your data
- Ask us to delete your data
- Opt out of data collection by not using the service

## Security

We protect your information using industry-standard security measures and encryption.
