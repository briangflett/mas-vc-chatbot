# Make a Difference in Your Community

If you are a dedicated and passionate person with advanced organizational skills who believes in not-for-profit organizations and the good that they can do, then MAS is the place for you.

[Join our volunteer team](https://www.masadvise.org/volunteers/#volunteer-application)

what we look for in a volunteer

## Volunteer With Us

MAS Volunteer Consultants use their management, business, technical and professional expertise to provide professional pro bono consulting for not-for-profit organizations in the GTA. You’ll bring your knowledge and experience into the community, work hand-in-hand with clients and provide practical and innovative solutions for clients. From Strategic and Business Planning, to enhancing Board Governance, Financial controls and Human Resource practices including Coaching and Volunteer Management, you’ll have an opportunity to engage in meaningful work that helps not-for-profit organizations maximize their resources, operate efficiently and build capacity.

In exchange for volunteering your knowledge and expertise for our clients, you will receive invaluable experience, and the satisfaction of knowing you have helped the amazing organizations in our community who help so many others.

## Requirements

- 5+ years experience and extensive knowledge and skills in the MAS Practice area (Strategic Planning, Governance, HR, Coaching, Volunteer Management, Finance, Marketing, Fundraising or IT)
- Individuals with 2+ years professional and relevant experience may apply to be a MAS Intern
- Capacity to work on a minimum of two projects per year (each project requires about 10 to 15 hours of your time)
- A desire to help not-for-profit organizations
- Ability to work independently
- Must be a Resident of Canada
