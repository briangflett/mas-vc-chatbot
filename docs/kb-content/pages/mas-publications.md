### Key Challenges Discussion Paper

[![](https://www.masadvise.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Key-Challenges-Discussion-Paper.png)](https://masadvise.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/MASCoordinator/EalNWWeVMINBgBJYu9rtHz0BIG8SQe-JANpLRWRPlcHr2Q?e=b8yRgt)

Periodically we carry out a formal survey of the organizations we’ve helped in order assess our relevance to them, and to identify the areas where NFPs will need the most help moving forward. Our 2024 survey results provide an interesting glimpse into the reality of the 255 organizations that sent in responses. The following paper provides a very brief summary of top line results and a discussion on some of the key challenges identified by respondents.
