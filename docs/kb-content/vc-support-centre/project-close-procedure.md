Initiating a MAS Project Close Procedure

In order to close a MAS Project, the VC must initiate the process. The steps are

VC fills in the VC section of the MAS Project Close form b y using this link https://masadvise.org/mas-project-close-form/

The VC uses the VC Save and Continue Later option at the bottom of the form to save their information

You MUST enter your email and click on Send Link after submitting the form. This will automatically send the link to the MAS Client Services Manager (CSM).

The CSM will send the link to the client with a request to close the project. The MAS PC will also close the project in the MAS database

A donation request is made in the email to the client.

You may choose to notify your client that the Project Close form will be sent to them by the CSM.
