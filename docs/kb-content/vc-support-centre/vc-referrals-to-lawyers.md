MAS VC Referrals to Lawyers.

From time to time MAS VC’s are asked by clients for answers to legal questions.

As you know MAS does not do anything that requires a licence. MAS VC’s do not give legal advice.

Often clients will then ask a VC for a referral to a lawyer.

MAS has referred many clients to the free legal service at Pro Bono Law Ontario with good results.

Please note that Pro Bono Law Ontario has recently modified their requirements for the types of organizations they will assist. Our contact, Nicole Kellow, indicates that they will be focusing on organizations whose mandate is to help the marginalized and improve the quality of life for fellow Ontarians. Pursued activities can include, but are not limited to: building affordable housing, creating jobs and economic growth in low-income neighborhoods, providing essential services to those with special needs, and protecting the environment. 

She adds that they recently launched a Free Legal Advice Hotline and you may be able to receive assistance via this service if your organization doesn’t fall into the categories described above.  When you call-in, she recommends contacting the lawyer via the Corporate/Non-Profit line option indicating the type of assistance you need.  They will advise you of next steps at that time. Here is the link for more information about this service.

PBLO has a roster of volunteer lawyers and they will seek to match the client’s need with the free appropriate legal counsel.

They have reviewed 

the final draft of a several MAS clients’ start up applications for NFP incorporation federally or provincially.

several of our NFP organizations applications to become a Registered Charity.

And they have referred NFP’s and Charities with complex legal questions to free counsel versed in these matters.

Since they depend on volunteer lawyer availability the length of time between initial request and legal consultation can vary, but recently a client reported their online application for service was answered within two business days and their consultation was within three weeks.

Nicole knows MAS well and is always pleased when any MAS clients indicate they heard about PBLO from MAS.

Don’t hesitate to give your client this referral if they are asking for legal advice and tell them to mention MAS.

Here is their profile below from the Law Society of Upper Canada Directory

Pro Bono Law Ontario (PBLO)

Pro Bono Ontario (PBO) promotes access to justice in Ontario by creating and promoting opportunities for lawyers to provide pro bono (free) legal services to persons of limited means. PBO develops projects individually for various law firms, legal departments and law associations. PBO also administers three streams of projects in-house to assist low-income Ontarians:

Law Help Ontario helps low-income unrepresented litigants with civil, NON-FAMILY matters

The Child Advocacy Project helps at-risk children and youth

Volunteer Lawyers Services helps charitable organizations with corporate law issues.

For the Public: Apply to speak to a pro bono lawyer

For Lawyers: Apply to volunteer
