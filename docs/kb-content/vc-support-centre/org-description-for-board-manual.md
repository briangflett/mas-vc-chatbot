2024 Organization Model:

We currently have 75 active Volunteer Consultants (April 2024).  These are recruited from Volunteer Toronto/Charity Village websites and/or by word of mouth.

On average our VCs carry out around 140 consulting projects each year

We receive between two and five Requests for Consulting Services each week, from a broad array of charities and not-for-profit organizations.

Our primary areas of expertise/consulting services are:

Governance

Planning and Strategic Planning

		Human Resources

		Finance and Information Technology

		Marketing and Communications (with advice around issues of Fundraising)

Every organisation ‘applicant’ completes the MAS Self-assessment Survey (Appendix A, attached) as part of intake in addition to providing a very basic introduction of their organization and a summary of the help they are seeking.

They are also asked to review and sign on to our MAS Client Terms and Conditions (Appendix B, attached)

Staff put together a weekly “Call-out” for VCs that summarizes the available projects.  VCs respond on a first-come, first-served basis though sometimes staff will reach out to an individual they think might be a great fit for an available project.

As per the Terms and Conditions once staff ‘introduce’ an interested VC with the applicant organization and they mutually decide to proceed with the project the VC becomes the volunteer of the organization, subject to their volunteer guidelines.  

For our records the VC will provide to MAS a summary of the workplan for the project agreed to with the organization.

When completed VCs are required to submit a Project Close form to MAS.  In turn this triggers a ‘we hope you enjoyed the project… please provide a brief evaluation… if you would please consider a donation” letter. NB: Client donations are MAS’s primary source of funding.

On occasion, in addition to the above, MAS will take on an internal project such as improving technology or developing resources/tools.  We also periodically offer on-line or in person workshops on a variety of topics of interest to the NFP sector.
